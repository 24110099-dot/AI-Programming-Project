# Acme Corp — International Work Policy (Section 8)

## Section 8.1: Working from Another Country

Employees may work from a company office in another country for up to 30 consecutive days. Work from another country requires prior approval from the line manager and the HR business partner. Work exceeding 30 consecutive days additionally requires approval from the department head and a tax review.

## Section 8.2: Required Documentation

A request to work from another country must state the destination, the start and end dates, the local office, and the business reason. Requests must be submitted at least ten business days in advance.

## Section 8.3: Tax and Compliance

Working from another country may create a tax presence in that country. HR must be notified before travel so that a tax review can be completed. Employees must not perform work that requires an export licence from a restricted country.

## Section 8.4: Expenses While Abroad

While working from another country, the standard travel expense rules apply. Meals are reimbursed up to $50 per day and lodging up to $160 per night. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
