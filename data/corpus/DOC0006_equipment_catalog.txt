# Northwind Systems — Equipment and Cost Reference

## EQ-01: Standard Field Kit

The standard field kit contains a laptop, a docking station, a headset, and a universal power adapter. Unit prices are not printed in this document; they are maintained in the equipment price list and must be looked up item by item.

## EQ-02: Daily Allowances

Meals are reimbursed up to $50 per day. Lodging is reimbursed up to $160 per night. Ground transport is reimbursed with receipts.

## EQ-03: Equipment Approval

Equipment purchases over $1,000 require approval from the department head. Purchases under $1,000 are approved by the team lead. All purchases must be recorded in the asset register within three working days of delivery.
