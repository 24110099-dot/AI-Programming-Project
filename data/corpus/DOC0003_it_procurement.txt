# Northwind Systems — Procurement and Expense Rules

## PR-01: Software Requests

Software requests are reviewed once a week by the procurement team. The decision is recorded on the request ticket and the requester is notified by email. Requests for software that duplicates a licence already held by the company are declined unless the requesting team provides a written business justification.

## PR-02: Hardware Purchases

Hardware purchases over $1,000 require approval from the department head before the order is placed. Purchases under $1,000 are approved by the team lead. All purchases must be recorded in the asset register within three working days of delivery.

## PR-03: Business Travel

Business travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal. Meals are reimbursed up to $50 per day and receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
