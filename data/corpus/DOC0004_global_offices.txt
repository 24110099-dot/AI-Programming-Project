# Northwind Systems — Global Office Directory

## GO-01: Office Locations

The company operates offices in Singapore, Vietnam (Ho Chi Minh City), Poland (Krakow), and the United States (Austin). Each office has a facilities owner and an IT owner. Desk allocations and local network access must be approved by both owners.

## GO-02: Vietnam Office — Ho Chi Minh City

The Ho Chi Minh City office is in District 1 and seats 40 people. Working from this office requires the APAC-VPN profile, a 220V-compatible power supply, and a booking in the visitor desk system. Desk allocations and local access are approved by the office's assigned facilities owner and IT owner; the current assignments and their contact addresses are held in the office directory, not in this document.

## GO-03: Regional IT Requirements

Staff working from any APAC office must use the APAC-VPN profile and enroll the device in the device-management programme. Access to production systems from an overseas office requires an additional security review, requested through the IT service desk.

## GO-04: Desk Booking Lead Time

Visitor desks must be booked at least five business days before arrival. Bookings are released automatically if the traveler does not confirm 48 hours before arrival.
